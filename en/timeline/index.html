Timeline

Before the studio,
and since.

Two periods carry the current thread: the institute years at CITIC Architectural Design & Research Institute, Wuhan studio (2021-2023), and the independent practice that followed.

  1. 2023-2026

    Independent practice

    DoraWolf Studio · Wuhan, China

    An independent practice working at the intersection of education, infrastructure, and public buildings. The studio reads each site, its topography, climate, and civic edges, before form is committed, then carries the design through a small kit of computational tools (Sketchup, Grasshopper, Rhino, Ladybug) until it has the precision to be built.

  2. 2021-2023

    Institute period

    CITIC Architectural Design & Research Institute · Wuhan studio · Wuhan, China

    Schematic and competition work across education, infrastructure, and civic typologies. The projects on the list above were largely conceived during these years and now form the studio's founding portfolio.
