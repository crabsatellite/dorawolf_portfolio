About the studio

DoraWolf Studio works in education, infrastructure, and public buildings. The method is single: read the site first, its contour, weather, and civic edges, then let form emerge from what is already there. Sustainable design and parametric modelling are the daily fabric of the practice, not a manifesto. The schemes that follow were drawn during 2021-2023 at CITIC Architectural Design & Research Institute, Wuhan studio. They cover schematic and competition work across education, infrastructure, and civic typologies, and are the studio's founding portfolio.

The studio reads each site, its topography, climate, and civic edges, before form is committed, then carries the design through a small kit of computational tools (Sketchup, Grasshopper, Rhino, Ladybug) until it has the precision to be built.
