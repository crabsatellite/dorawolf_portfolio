## Brief

A concept-stage proposal for a 48-class senior high school campus at the southern edge of Hongshan District, adjacent to Qingling Lake.

## Concept

Existing topography is preserved as a series of stepped terraces. Instruction occupies the upper plateau, athletic and student-life programs are draped down the slope, and the lakeside is left as a continuous public path. The scheme keeps cut-and-fill close to net-zero across the site.
