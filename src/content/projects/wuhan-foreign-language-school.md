## Brief

The school occupies a parcel at the eastern edge of Wuhan's Yangtze New District master plan, with frontages on a future arterial road and a planned linear park. The brief asks for a 60-class K-12 campus alongside athletic facilities, a 600-seat assembly hall, and a residential block for boarding students.

## Concept

Three figures organise the plan: a teaching strip, a public spine, and a perimeter. Classrooms are stacked north of the spine to harvest stable daylight; assembly, library, and exhibition hang from the spine itself. A perimeter walk wraps the playing fields and reads, from the city, as a single quiet datum.
