## Brief

A combined operations center for the Wuhan Metro line serving the National Convention Center, with public-facing exhibition and a rooftop observation deck overlooking the new transit interchange.

## Concept

A horizontal datum runs the length of the site and lifts to reveal the operational floors. The public route enters from a softened plaza on the south and rises to the deck without crossing operational zones — security, dispatch, and maintenance are kept on a separate vertical core to the north.
